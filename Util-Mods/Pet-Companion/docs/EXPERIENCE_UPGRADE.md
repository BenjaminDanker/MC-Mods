# Pet experience upgrade

## Deployment

Apply all forward MariaDB migrations through V016 in version order before starting the updated service. V009 adds memory provenance and query indexes; V010 adds durable decision windows; V011 persists fatigue and awake time; V012 binds decisions to bounded intentions; V013 records relationship evidence; V014 adds organic companion context and open threads; V015 corrects dialogue-event provenance; and V016 records affect provenance and thread update times. The service does not migrate its database at startup. Existing `PET_DIALOGUE_MODEL` and `PET_CONSOLIDATION_MODEL` overrides still work; their defaults are `gpt-6-luna`. The sparse planner uses GPT-6 Luna. The embedding model remains `text-embedding-3-small`.

The standard short-context GPT-6 Luna price defaults are $0.10 input, $0.01 cached input, and $0.50 output per million tokens ([official model pricing](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-6-luna)). Dialogue, planning, consolidation, and embeddings all reserve and record usage against the existing subscriber budget. A lapse, budget exhaustion, provider outage, or disabled AI leaves local following and deterministic actions available.

The shared Chat Completions inference client sends `reasoning_effort: none` and no `temperature` field for dialogue, consolidation, or sparse decisions. It therefore uses the API default temperature of 1 rather than a configured value. GPT-6 Luna supports `temperature` with `reasoning_effort: none`; the parameter is unsupported when reasoning effort is higher ([OpenAI model guidance](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/latest-model), [Chat Completions reference](https://platform.openai.com/docs/api-reference/chat/create)). Embeddings do not use temperature.

## Behavior and data boundaries

The loaded Fabric entity records a small set of experiences only while its owner is nearby in the same world. It samples visible owner block breaks and mob defeats with long cooldowns; owner actions while the pet is distant, unloaded, or out of sight remain unknown. A bounded background transport sends events without network I/O on the Minecraft tick thread. The service verifies the owner, backend, dimension, and exact materialized entity against SQL before accepting an event. The event has a unique ID, timestamp, importance, and server-derived summary. Local and SQL debounce prevent repeated threats, interactions, and area visits from flooding context. Recent verified events appear in dialogue with their time and event ID. No event is inferred across a server boundary or unloaded chunk.

SQL owns placement, mood, traits, events, and memory cards. Qdrant supplies bounded similarity candidates only; each candidate is hydrated and checked against the active SQL card and version. Ranking uses semantic score (55%), query terms (20%), recency (10%), importance (10%), and matching location (5%). A place named in the owner's question takes precedence over the current location. Backend names distinguish Vanilla, Ocean, Sky Islands, Desert, Cave, and Magic worlds; dimension IDs distinguish Overworld, Nether, and The End; biome IDs distinguish places within them. These IDs ground recall and normally stay out of pet speech unless the owner asks or the place matters. Location adds relevance but never excludes a different world. SQL fallback scans a bounded card set and ranks by query terms and context. Repeated equivalent queries use a small short-lived embedding cache, and cheap recent/SQL matches can skip the query embedding. Ordinary actions never request embeddings. Only durable cards chosen during sleep consolidation enter the embedding queue. Held and unloaded pets continue SQL sleep transitions, eligible consolidation, and card embeddings without a physical entity. Active sleep cycles replay job enqueue on service restart.

Cards distinguish verified game observations, owner statements, and inferences, and retain source events and timestamps. Consolidation requires an ordered factual word span from a cited event, including negation, so an invented value or reversed preference cannot pass a word-overlap check. This conservative rule may reject valid paraphrases. A later owner preference with the same fact key retires the earlier card in SQL; vector cleanup follows, while SQL verification immediately prevents retired results from being recalled. Mood decays in bounded steps toward trait/species baselines, including while held or unloaded. Fatigue is a separate SQL value that controls genuine sleep and updates temporary tired mood at sleep/activity transitions.

Dog and cat adapters use vanilla navigation, look control, sitting poses, and their ambient sound choice. The managed pet's random ambient call is suppressed. The action controller plays a species-appropriate call when greeting for attention or when a pet utterance is displayed, with cooldowns; ordinary wolves/cats and gameplay sounds are unaffected. The default follow target is 3.5 blocks from the owner, with following starting at six blocks, to leave room for the conversation billboard. The adapters have safe fallbacks for a mob lacking a capability. Following, owner movement, danger, and transfer interrupt sitting and nearby exploration. Investigation stays within eight blocks of the owner, about five blocks from the pet, and at most one chunk from the owner's chunk at simulation distance four. It requires an already loaded target chunk and allocates zero chunk tickets. No optional action requests combat, teleportation, permission changes, or chunk loading. Managed pets reject all incoming Minecraft damage and pet-attributed outgoing damage; invalid or void-fallen entities are removed for loaded-only authority recovery.

The local action controller runs on loaded, awake pets and chooses a new action after a bounded window without an LLM call. A per-window hash spreads choices through normal play; curiosity, attachment, independence, anxiety, and species bias investigation and approach. Sits have a one-minute cooldown and last up to twenty seconds, while investigations last at most eight seconds and try several safe, already loaded nearby targets. Owner movement and distance interrupt both. An explicit owner request accepted in a successful conversation can suggest sit, stand, rest, return, investigation, or a call; Fabric still applies capability and follow safety rules. The sparse planner considers novel verified arrivals, areas, survived danger, or a return after a long witnessed absence. It has durable per-pet windows, one in-flight request per pet, a global concurrency limit and spacing, a short timeout, bounded structured intents and expiry, and budget admission. Routine greetings, movement, sound, and event bursts run locally. A sparse event decision can yield a short unsolicited utterance without a right-click; ordinary back-and-forth dialogue opens on owner interaction. Direct dialogue receives current place IDs when the owner asks about place or travel, immediate danger and injury when observed, and ordinary activity only when relevant. Recent witnessed events, prior turns, mood, traits, and ranked memories remain available. Unknown owner facts remain unknown.

Walk-through MCServerPortals already reserves a nearby placed pet before its signed proxy request. Warden, Wither, Elder Guardian, End, and Babylon redirects now do the same through the shared portal protocol. SQL uses `TRANSFERRING` while the physical entity is absent, then the final backend claims and places one entity near the owner. A pet already `HELD` stays held. A pet outside the 16-block carry radius, in another dimension, or unloaded remains on its authoritative source; it is never silently claimed by the destination. Failed or interrupted reservations expire to `HELD`, and the sleeping flag remains in SQL through transfer. No portal action adds a chunk ticket. Actual cross-server portal activation still needs a player in-game.

## Need-based sleep and cost

The service adds six fatigue points per online awake hour and one per offline hour. Verified, deduplicated activity adds one to eight points. At 60 points the pet can nap; at 85 points or 12 hours awake it takes a longer sleep. A 16-hour awake deadline always applies. A 75-minute minimum awake period prevents immediate repeat naps. A normal nap lasts 12 real minutes and recovers 45 points; a long sleep lasts 40 minutes and recovers 85. The first 30-minute network absence starts a nap, while later offline rest continues under the awake deadline. Every value is configurable through `PET_SLEEP_*` in `config/pet-service.env.example`; the awake deadline is capped below 24 hours. A real sleep uses SQL `SLEEPING`, stops navigation, and shows a supported sitting pose. Brief `REST` or `SIT` actions never launch consolidation.

Only cycles with meaningful pending events enqueue a consolidation job. The unique sleep-cycle key and memory-card/version embedding key prevent duplicate work after retries or restart. No ordinary movement, sound, rest, or investigation requests inference or embeddings. GPT-6 Luna remains the default for eligible dialogue/consolidation and the sparse planner; the embedding model is separate. The existing subscription and period budget guard every provider call. At the current default maximum reservation (6,000 input and 800 output tokens), one consolidation call reserves at most about $0.001. Even 90 meaningful cycles in a month would reserve about $0.09; empty naps reserve $0. Embeddings are charged per durable card, not per observed event.

## In-game check

Run with the updated service, migrations, and one dog and one cat on a test backend. For each species: place the pet, walk away and return to see greeting/approach and confirm the pet stops with at least three blocks of billboard clearance; travel together to another world and ask about a witnessed experience in the earlier world; ask about an owner action that happened out of sight and check that the pet does not claim to have seen it; wait for a sit and then move away to verify prompt following; watch a brief nearby investigation return to the owner without forced chunks; listen for one appropriate call when the pet greets for attention or displays spoken output, without overlapping random ambient calls; observe a genuine 12-minute nap and wake; pick up the pet during sleep and place it again before wake to confirm the SQL pose is restored; leave it held or its chunk unloaded through another cycle and inspect SQL/job/embedding progress; lapse the test subscription to check physical behavior continues while paid calls stop; and transfer to another backend to confirm the source disappears and only the destination pet acts. Test attacks, fire, lava, drowning, fall, void, and pet attacks against a wild mob; ordinary wild animals should remain damageable.

## Recorded verification

A synthetic Dev Player run on 2026-10-04 verified source-linked inference from
three distinct witnessed events, preservation of witnessed location metadata,
hybrid SQL/vector recall when a relevant card was not indexed, and pet-scoped
sleep/consolidation work that left another test pet's jobs untouched. The
recorded candidate build passed 238 unit tests and all 29 required Fabric
GameTests. These are historical, isolated checks, not a live deployment or a
player-experience claim.

## Verification limits

Before release, rerun the current build, tests, GameTests, and migrations against
isolated data. A player must still verify visible movement, sound, sleep, and
real portal behavior in game. Optional exploration is intentionally brief and
cannot solve paths through unloaded terrain; existing compass, pickup, recall,
and transfer flows handle those cases. Visible sleep transitions can lag the
SQL transition by the scheduler and authority reconciliation intervals.
